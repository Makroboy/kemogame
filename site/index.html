PICK UP & PLAY

How to play

Collect all the musical notes in the level. Avoid the wrong notes fired by the loudspeakers.

On a computer, use a keyboard or click the on-screen buttons. On a phone or tablet, use the touch controls. The instructions below cover Windows PCs, Macs, iPads, iPhones and Android devices.

Start a game

  1. Click or tap Start game and wait for the game to load. This takes a moment, especially the first time.
  2. Click or tap Menu or press M to open Options.
  3. Select New game with the keyboard or on-screen ↑ / ↓ buttons and press Enter or tap OK.
  4. Choose a character with the keyboard or on-screen ← / → buttons, then press Enter or tap OK to play.

Phones, tablets and touchscreens

Use the arrow buttons to move and jump, OK to confirm, and Menu / Back for the game’s menu actions. The labels at the bottom of the game screen show their current functions.

On a touchscreen, hold ← or → and tap ↑ with another finger to jump while moving.

Full screen hides the phone and enlarges the game. On a phone or tablet, play in portrait orientation.

After completing a level

Press Enter / OK to skip the level-clear celebration, or let it finish automatically. If you return to the title screen, use Menu → New game to open character selection. Choose another character with ← / → and press Enter / OK. A character marked Level clear has already been completed.

Original sound

The title screen has its own tune. During play, collecting a note or taking a hit triggers a short sound; there is no continuous gameplay backing track in the original game. Completing a character’s level plays that character’s tune. Game over and completing all characters have separate tunes. Use Sound: on in the game’s Options menu and press a game control to enable browser audio.

Original phone controls

The number keys still work: 4 / 6 to move, 1 / 2 / 3 to jump, and 5 to confirm. Open Original keypad below the controls to show the Nokia keypad.

Press a game control to enable audio. Internet connection required for the browser runtime.

A FINNISH MOBILE GAMING CURIOSITY

The story of KemoGame

A band, a sliding Nokia and a small piece of mobile gaming history.

Mobile Kemopetrol · 2002

In 2002, Kemopetrol took part in the Mobile Kemopetrol project, led by Hewlett-Packard’s Mobile E-Services Bazaar. As part of the project, Sumea created KemoGame, a J2ME platform game about the band for the Nokia 7650.

It appears to have been a demonstration concept for adding value for people who bought the record. At the time, record labels were fighting piracy and looking for new ways to encourage people to buy music.

Players collect musical notes while dodging mad loudspeakers that fire wrong notes. Each member of Kemopetrol became a playable character.

More about the developer: Sumea’s website from 2004.

Goodbye: a streaming music video

The Mobile Kemopetrol project also included the release of a streamable music video for the band’s song Goodbye. According to the band’s account of the project, it was the world’s first streamable music video.

The Mobile Kemopetrol website

The kemopetrolmusic.com website was created specifically for the Mobile Kemopetrol activities. Alongside this project site, the band also ran and fully managed its own websites, kemopetrol.org and kemopetrol.com.

Back out of the phone

Recovering the game from the old handset took some persistence: buying a replacement battery, finding a two-way infrared dongle, installing Nokia PC Suite on a Windows computer, and installing a file-access application on the Nokia 7650. Eventually, the original files made it safely onto a computer.

This browser edition brings those original game files back into play.

The original archive

The recovered package contains KemoGame.jar, the game program with its graphics, characters, sounds and level data; KemoGame.jad, the installation descriptor; original Symbian installation metadata; and rms.db, the original record-store database for scores and settings.

KemoGame was built for MIDP 1.0 / CLDC 1.0.
